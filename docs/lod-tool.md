### To get access to SoCo (Social Coding) repositories, you can follow these steps:

1.  Go to the IT Service Portal: [IT Service Portal](https://service-management.bosch.tech/sp?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=ae0fa1bb1b87551078087403dd4bcbf2) <!-- WARNING: Unmapped Confluence link - please update manually -->
2.  Click on "Security Groups / Distribution Lists" and select "Order for Self" (or "Order for others" if you want to request access for a colleague).
3.  In the search field, enter "rb\_sde\_soco\_da\_developer\_uf" and click on the search symbol.
4.  Choose the security group "rb\_sde\_soco\_da\_developer\_uf" that appears in the search results.
5.  Click on "Add Member" and insert your name. You can either insert your Bosch/Windows-UserID (e.g., hje2lr) or insert "Lastname Firstname".
6.  Select any responsible approver (who is not on vacation) and press "Submit".
